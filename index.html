ABOUT NEURALFORGE AI

From classroom concepts to industry-ready skills.

NeuralForge AI is a training institute founded by IISc alumni, focused on helping students build strong foundations in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Generative AI.

Our approach emphasizes understanding concepts, writing code, solving real problems and completing projects that students can confidently discuss in interviews.
